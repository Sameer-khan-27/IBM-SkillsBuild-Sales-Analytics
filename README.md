## 📌 Executive Summary
This capstone investigates 1,000 real-world retail transactions recorded across supermarket branches in Yangon, Naypyitaw, and Mandalay. Utilizing an end-to-end data analytics workflow in Python, the study assesses product line contributions, customer purchasing behaviors (Loyalty Members vs. Regular Walk-ins), checkout payment channels, and store unit performances to provide operational insights.

## 📈 Key Strategic Findings
1. **Balanced Category Revenue:** *Food & Beverages* ($56,145) and *Sports & Travel* ($55,123) lead gross sales. The remaining four categories generate between $49K and $54K each, reflecting stable inventory turnover.
2. **Branch Sales Consistency:** Naypyitaw generated the highest volume ($110,569 / 34.2%), while Yangon ($106,200) and Mandalay ($106,198) tied with ~32.9% market share each.
3. **Cashless Preference:** Cash accounts for only 34.4% of total payments, indicating high customer readiness for dedicated self-checkout terminals.
